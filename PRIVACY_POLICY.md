# Privacy Policy — Eagle Pathway

**Effective date:** 5 June 2026
**Last updated:** 5 June 2026

Eagle Pathway ("we", "us", "the app") helps students in Ethiopia and across Africa
find scholarships, prepare applications, and connect with tutors. This policy
explains what personal data we collect, why, how it is stored, and the choices
you have. By creating an account you agree to this policy.

> **Before publishing:** replace `support@eaglepathway.app` below with your real
> contact address, host this page at a public URL, and enter that URL in the
> Google Play Console (Data safety → Privacy policy) and in the app listing.

---

## 1. Who we are
Eagle Pathway is operated by the Eagle Pathway team. For any privacy question or
request, contact us at **support@eaglepathway.app**.

## 2. Data we collect

**You provide directly**
- **Account details:** full name, email address, phone number, and password
  (passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us).
- **Profile / academic details:** city, grade level, GPA, intended degree level,
  fields and countries of interest, English-proficiency status, academic summary,
  and career goals.
- **Documents you upload:** e.g. transcripts, degree certificates, passport, CV,
  IELTS certificates, statements of purpose, and recommendation letters.
- **Content you generate:** scholarship applications, statements of purpose,
  tutor bookings, messages, and payment/receipt information you submit.
- **AI assistant input:** text you send to the in-app assistant, SOP review, or
  mock-interview features.

**Collected automatically**
- **Push notification token** (to deliver reminders and updates).
- **Basic app/diagnostic data** needed to operate the service (e.g. error logs).

**Device permissions we may request**
- **Camera & storage / photos:** only to let you upload documents and a profile photo.
- **Notifications:** to send deadline reminders and status updates.
These are used only for the stated purpose and only after you grant them.

## 3. How we use your data
- Create and manage your account and profile.
- Match you with scholarships and tutors and process applications and bookings.
- Review documents and provide consulting/tutoring services.
- Provide AI-assisted SOP review, an assistant, and mock interviews.
- Send reminders and notifications you have enabled (you can turn these off in Settings).
- Process payments and payouts and keep required transaction records.
- Maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with law.

We do **not** sell your personal data, and we do **not** use it for third-party advertising.

## 4. Where your data is stored and who processes it
Your data is stored using trusted service providers acting on our behalf:
- **Supabase** — database, authentication, and file storage for your account,
  profile, and documents.
- **Groq** — processes the text you submit to AI features (SOP review, assistant,
  mock interview) to generate responses. Submit only information you are
  comfortable sharing with an AI processor.
- **Expo / Google** — delivery of push notifications.
- **Vercel** — hosting of our backend API used by the AI features.

These providers may process data on servers outside your country. We share data
with them only as needed to run the service.

## 5. Data retention
We keep your data while your account is active. You can request deletion of your
account and associated personal data at any time (see Section 7). We may retain
limited records where required for legal, accounting, or fraud-prevention purposes.

## 6. Security
We use industry-standard measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS),
authenticated access, and database row-level security so that you can only access
your own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your
information.

## 7. Your rights and choices
- **Access / correction:** view and edit your profile in the app.
- **Notifications:** enable or disable each type in Settings.
- **Account & data deletion:** request deletion in the app (Settings → Delete
  Account) or by emailing **support@eaglepathway.app**. We will delete your
  personal data except where retention is legally required.

## 8. Children
Eagle Pathway is intended for prospective and current students. If you are a minor,
use the app only with the involvement of a parent or guardian. Parents may link to
a student account to support and monitor their child's progress.

## 9. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by
updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.

## 10. Contact
Questions or requests: **support@eaglepathway.app**
